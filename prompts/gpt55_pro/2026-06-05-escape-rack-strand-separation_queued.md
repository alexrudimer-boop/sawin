Prompt:

```text
Please answer self-containedly and mathematically. Do not give only a bounded
computation. The task is to prove or refute a specific orbit-separation lemma
arising from minimal-ideal transport groupoids for finite bijective
set-theoretic Yang-Baxter solutions.

Context:

Let X be a finite bijective YBE solution, written

    r(x,y)=(L_x(y),R_y(x)).

We are focused on the everywhere-singular residual case, where every L_x and
every R_x is non-bijective.  Let

    S_L=<L_x:x in X>,    S_R=<R_y:y in X>

be the nonempty-product left and right transformation semigroups.  Let G_L and
G_R be their minimal-ideal transport groupoids: objects are minimal-rank images
of idempotents in the minimal ideals, and morphisms are restrictions of
minimal-ideal elements between such images.

The combined two-sided groupoid is

    C = G_L x G_R^op.

For A in Ob(G_L), B in Ob(G_R), define the elementary strand transport

    eta(x;A,B) = (L_x|_A, (R_x|_B)^op),

an arrow of C

    eta(x;A,B): (A,R_x B) -> (L_x A,B).

For a local crossing r(x,y)=(u,v), and external minimal images A,B, define

    a  = eta(x;L_y A,B),
    b  = eta(y;A,R_x B),
    a' = eta(u;L_v A,B),
    b' = eta(v;A,R_u B).

The YBE product identities give

    L_u L_v = L_x L_y,
    R_v R_u = R_y R_x,

so the old and new local composites agree in C:

    a' b' = a b

with the chosen composition convention.  A rack crossing needs more:

    (a,b) -> (a*b,a).

Now choose a base object o of C and connectors tau_P:o -> P.  Put

    H = Aut_C(o).

Every arrow alpha:P -> Q has normalized label

    nu(alpha)=tau_Q^{-1} alpha tau_P in H.

Identify arrows with triples (Q,g,P).  Define the finite action rack

    Y_0 = Arr(C)

by

    (Q,g,P) * (Q',h,P') = (Q', g h g^{-1}, P').

Let ~_esc be the least rack congruence on Y_0 generated by the elementary
escape relations

    b' ~ a,
    a' ~ a*b

for all local crossings and all external boundary pairs.  Let

    Y_esc = Y_0 / ~_esc,
    q:Y_0 -> Y_esc.

Then every elementary crossing satisfies

    q(a') = q(a)*q(b),
    q(b') = q(a).

For arity n and word w=(x_1,...,x_n), choose boundary objects A in O_L,
B in O_R, and propagate cuts

    A_n=A,          A_{i-1}=L_{x_i}(A_i),
    B_0=B,          B_i=R_{x_i}(B_{i-1}).

Define

    eta_i^{A,B}(w)=eta(x_i;A_i,B_{i-1}).

Taking all boundary pairs Omega=O_L x O_R at once, set

    Y = Y_esc^Omega

and define

    Psi_n(w)_i =
        ( q(eta_i^{A,B}(w)) )_{(A,B) in Omega}
        in Y.

The local escape relations imply

    Psi_n:X^n -> Y^n

is B_n-equivariant.

The exact missing lemma is:

Minimal-ideal strand-separation lemma.

    If w' is in the B_n-orbit of w and Psi_n(w')=Psi_n(w), then w'=w.

If this lemma holds for X, then Sawin domination follows for X: take the inert
orbit observer I_n=X^n/B_n and

    Phi_n(w)=(Psi_n(w),[w]).

This is B_n-equivariant and injective.  Hence ker rho^Y_n <= ker rho^X_n for
all n.

Task:

Audit and resolve this conditional construction as far as possible.

1. First check the construction itself:
   - Is C=G_L x G_R^op the correct orientation?
   - Are the arrows eta(x;A,B) and the local composites a,b,a',b' correctly
     typed?
   - Does a'b'=ab really follow from L_uL_v=L_xL_y and R_vR_u=R_yR_x at the
     level of restricted groupoid arrows?
   - Is Y_0 a rack under the stated operation?
   - Does quotienting by the least rack congruence generated by b'~a and
     a'~a*b give the claimed local rack equivariance?

2. Then address the strand-separation lemma:
   - Prove it under explicit hypotheses that are natural for a rigid core, or
   - Give a conceptual counterexample, or
   - Reduce failure to a concrete structural feature such as a quotient,
     crossing-closed subsolution, invariant observer, flip-across/twisted
     union, or active factor.

3. If the lemma is false as stated, produce the exact corrected theorem:
   - What finite rack labels should replace Y_esc?
   - Or what additional boundary/object/path state is necessary?
   - Or what explicit same-orbit collision w != w' with Psi_n(w)=Psi_n(w')
     blocks every detector of this minimal-ideal escape-rack form?

4. Explain whether a minimal rigid-core counterexample must defeat this
   maximal escape-rack detector at arbitrarily large arities, and how that
   interacts with fixed-arity rack cofinality.

Important constraints:

- Do not claim Sawin is proved unless the orbit-separation lemma is proved for
  all finite residual cores.
- Do not replace orbit-injectivity by global injectivity; the inert orbit
  observer already separates different braid orbits.
- Do not claim equivariant surjection is equivalent to kernel containment.
- If the construction fails, identify the exact first failed equation or type
  mismatch.
- If the lemma holds only under extra hypotheses, state those hypotheses
  precisely and say whether they follow from the rigid-core filters.
```
