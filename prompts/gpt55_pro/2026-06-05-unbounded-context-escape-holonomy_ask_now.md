# Unbounded Context Escape Holonomy Prompt

Status: ask_now / prepared for GPT-5.5 Pro on 2026-06-05.

Prompt:

```text
Please answer self-containedly and mathematically.  We are working on Sawin's
finite rack-domination problem:

    For every finite bijective set-theoretic YBE solution X, does there exist
    a finite rack Y such that ker rho_n^Y <= ker rho_n^X for every n?

Current route: finite contextual rack detectors.

Recent boundary.

For a fixed finite quotient theta:L_X->M, actual contextual endpoint labels of
Theta_n^M lie in the finite set

    S_M={d_{a,x,b}:a,b in M, x in X}.

If every distinct pair in S_M is separated by some finite rack quotient of
C_M(X), then one finite product quotient is injective on all of S_M.  After
that, fixed-M bad pairs are exactly those with identical Theta_n^M tuples.

Therefore a shift-rack/factorial obstruction cannot be a fixed-M endpoint-label
obstruction.  Factorial holonomy can exist ambiently in As(C_M(X)), as in the
one-point/free-quandle example, but it is harmless unless it is reachable by
actual same-orbit bad pairs.

The remaining possible obstruction is not fixed-`M` endpoint factoriality, but
escape from fixed finite endpoint control:

    unbounded arity/refinement escape of exact finite contextual labels,
    or hidden profinite path holonomy.

This can happen in at least two finite-symbolic ways:

    (1) fixed-M unbounded arity:
        for some fixed finite M, after S_M is endpoint-separated, exact
        Theta_n^M-label collisions persist in arbitrarily large arity;

    (2) state-refinement escape:
        every fixed finite M is eventually defeated, so finer and finer
        quotients of L_X are needed.

Hidden path holonomy can also remain as profinite transporter/stabilizer-coset
data even when endpoint labels are identical.

Definitions to use.

For fixed M, let the reachable escape groupoid E_M(X) have objects reachable
pointed contextual labels (x,i) or their labels

    kappa_i^M(x)=d_{a_i,x_i,b_i} in S_M,

and morphisms generated by braid moves following a marked strand.  There is a
functor from E_M(X) to the action groupoid of As(C_M(X)) acting on C_M(X),
recording the escape cocycle/transporter.

Task.

1. Formalize the reachable escape groupoid E_M(X).  Define objects,
   generating morphisms, composition, the endpoint label map, and the cocycle
   into As(C_M(X)).  Make precise which transporters are actually reachable
   from braid dynamics.

2. Prove or refute the fixed-M endpoint lemma in full detail:
   after finite-rack separating S_M, every remaining fixed-M bad pair has
   identical Theta_n^M labels.  Does any hidden stabilizer holonomy remain
   relevant to finite rack detection once endpoints are identical?

3. Analyze the two escape modes separately.
   - Fixed-M unbounded arity: if a fixed M leaves exact Theta^M-label
     collisions in arbitrarily large arity, can bounded witnesses, automata,
     or braid locality reduce them?
   - State-refinement escape: if every fixed M is defeated, can this be
     represented as a profinite relation on hat{L_X} x X x hat{L_X}?  What
     extra condition would make it finite-index?

4. Try to construct a harmful ultrafilter whose obstruction escapes fixed
   endpoint control:
   - either exact Theta^M-label collisions persist for one fixed M in
     unbounded arity;
   - or, for each finite M, there are same-orbit bad pairs with identical
     Theta^M labels, but every fixed M is eventually defeated by state
     refinement.
   Can either happen in a finite YBE solution?

5. If construction fails, identify the mechanism.  Possibilities:
   - bounded harmful witnesses;
   - finite-index contextual Myhill-Nerode behavior;
   - reachable transporters have uniformly separable cosets;
   - unbounded context collapse yields a proper domination-reducing active
     factor.

6. Analyze active-factor extraction specifically from fixed-M unbounded arity,
   state-refinement escape, and hidden path holonomy.  Given exact contextual
   collisions or profinite relations on contexts, when do they yield finite
   maps pi_{a,b}:X->Z satisfying functionality, totality, bijectivity, YBE on
   all triples, and ker rho_n^Z <= ker rho_n^X?

7. Analyze product/padding again under the fixed-M lemma.  Prove that
   one-point or trivial-action padding contributes only inert escape holonomy
   or a proper domination-equivalent active factor, and cannot be residual-rigid
   harmful behavior.

8. State the strongest valid theorem.  Useful outcomes include:
   - reachable escape-holonomy uniformity theorem;
   - finite-index contextual Myhill-Nerode theorem;
   - active-factor extraction theorem for fixed-M unbounded arity or
     state-refinement escape;
   - explicit harmful unbounded-context obstruction;
   - or a sharper missing lemma strictly smaller than Sawin.

Guardrails:

- Do not confuse ambient As(C_M(X)) holonomy with reachable escape holonomy.
- Do not treat fixed-M factorial transporter families as endpoint obstructions
  once S_M is finite-rack separated.
- Do not claim pointwise separation gives one finite state quotient.
- Do not use ordinary quotients as active factors without proving
  ker rho_n^Z <= ker rho_n^X.
- Do not assume profinite context collapse has finite index without proof.
- Do not assume harmfulness must use finer and finer M; fixed-M exact
  contextual-label collisions in unbounded arity are still possible.
```
