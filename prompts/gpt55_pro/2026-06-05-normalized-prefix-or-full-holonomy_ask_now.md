# Normalized Prefix Or Full Holonomy Prompt

Status: ask_now / prepared for GPT-5.5 Pro on 2026-06-05.

Prompt:

```text
Please answer self-containedly and mathematically. Do not give only a bounded
computation. We are working on Sawin's finite rack-domination problem:

    For every finite bijective set-theoretic YBE solution X, does there exist
    a finite rack Y such that ker rho_n^Y <= ker rho_n^X for every n?

Write r_X(x,y)=(L_x(y),R_y(x)).

Known guardrails:

1. Product observers are inert but kernel-universal by singular padding:
   X=E x Z has ker rho_n^X=ker rho_n^Z.

2. Product-Hurwitz separability is a valid sufficient theorem, but finite
   residual separation of the suffix Hurwitz racks U_L(X), U_R(X) is an extra
   hypothesis.

3. Prefix-path rack vertexization is a valid sufficient theorem.  Let
   S=S_L^1.  If a finite rack Y and maps psi:S x X -> Y satisfy, for
   r_X(x,y)=(u,v),

       psi(p,u)=psi(pL_x,y),                              (V1)
       psi(pL_u,v)=psi(pL_x,y) triangleright psi(p,x),    (V2)

   and the induced prefix labels plus inert observers are injective in every
   arity, then ker rho_n^Y <= ker rho_n^X for every n.

4. The universal prefix rack V_L(X) generated by a_{p,x} with relations
   (VL1),(VL2) collapses through evaluation:

       a_{p,x}=a_{1,p(x)}.

   Thus V_L(X) is the normalized prefix rack W_L(X), generated by b_z, z in X,
   with relations

       b_{pL_u(v)} = b_{p(u)} triangleright b_{p(x)}

   for all p in S and r_X(x,y)=(u,v).

5. Every V_L(X)-based prefix vertexization factors through the normalized
   prefix map

       D_n(x_1,...,x_n)
       =
       (x_1, L_{x_1}(x_2), ..., L_{x_1}...L_{x_{n-1}}(x_n)).

   Hence if w != w' lie in the same braid orbit and D_n(w)=D_n(w'), no finite
   quotient of V_L(X) can separate them.

6. Finite-rack residual equivalence in W_L(X) is profinite.  Let
   G_L(X)=As(W_L(X)) and H_z=Stab_{G_L(X)}(b_z).  If b_{z'}=g.b_z, then

       z ==_fin z'
       iff
       g in closure_prof(H_z).

Current barriers:

    finite/evaluative:
        D_n may fail to separate distinct points inside braid orbits;

    profinite:
        relevant stabilizers H_z in As(W_L(X)) may fail to be profinitely
        closed on needed cosets.

Task.

1. Audit the collapse V_L(X) ~= W_L(X).  Is it unavoidable for any one-rack
   prefix vertexization satisfying (V1),(V2), or is there a modified finite
   rack construction that retains full arrows p --x--> pL_x while still giving
   a braid-kernel detector?

2. Analyze the normalized prefix map D_n.
   - Is D_n injective on braid orbits for residual rigid cores with no proper
     domination-reducing active factor?
   - If not, does a same-orbit collision D_n(w)=D_n(w') force a proper active
     factor, an inert observer branch, or another known positive branch?
   - If no, give the sharpest obstruction or finite YBE table pattern.

3. Analyze W_L(X) and G_L(X)=As(W_L(X)).
   - Do residual rigid hypotheses imply profinite separability of the relevant
     stabilizers H_z?
   - Can arbitrary finitely presented Wirtinger-type subgroup-separability
     problems be realized by G_L(X), making this route too strong?
   - Are there known classes of YBE solutions for which G_L(X) is virtually
     polycyclic, virtually free, linear with separable stabilizers, or
     otherwise subgroup separable?

4. Try to build a full-prefix-arrow detector.  The full prefix path groupoid
   has arrows p --x--> pL_x and retains Rees sandwich data, but a rack crossing
   requires the first output label to equal the second input label, forcing
   (p,u)=(pL_x,y) at the rack-label level.  Is there a way to encode the full
   arrow data using:
   - a larger rack with additional state;
   - a product of rack labels and inert observers;
   - a state-dependent active factor Z;
   - or a different finite rack detector not governed by (V1),(V2)?

5. State the strongest valid reduced theorem.  Possibilities include:
   - normalized-prefix orbit faithfulness plus stabilizer separability implies
     domination;
   - a finite quotient of As(W_L(X)) with coset rack separating all needed
     normalized prefix labels implies domination;
   - a coherent finite state-dependent active factor pi_p:X->Z implies
     domination.

6. Decide whether this route can still plausibly finish Sawin, or whether the
   normalized-prefix/profinite barriers are independent obstructions.

Important:

- Do not claim product observers alone prove domination.
- Do not use ordinary quotients as active factors unless ker rho_n^Z <=
  ker rho_n^X is proved.
- Do not claim V_L(X) retains full prefix holonomy after (VL1).
- Do not replace profinite subgroup separability with finite semigroup closure.
- A useful answer should either prove the normalized-prefix/profinite barriers
  harmless, refute that with a realizable obstruction, or propose a genuinely
  stronger full-arrow rack detector.
```
