```text
Please answer self-containedly and mathematically. Do not give only a bounded

We are working on Sawin's finite rack-domination problem for finite bijective
set-theoretic Yang-Baxter solutions X:

    Does there exist a finite rack Y such that
    ker rho_n^Y <= ker rho_n^X for every n?

Write

    r_X(x,y)=(L_x(y),R_y(x)).

Known status:

1. Product observers

    Lambda_n(x_1,...,x_n)=L_{x_1}...L_{x_n}

and the right analogue Gamma_n are genuine inert observers, but they do not
reduce Sawin.  For any finite solution Z and any finite identity solution E
with |E|>1, the product X=E x Z is everywhere singular and

    ker rho_n^X = ker rho_n^Z

for every n.  Thus product observers alone are kernel-universal.

2. Product-Hurwitz separability is a valid sufficient theorem, but residual
separability is not proved.  The universal left Hurwitz rack U_L(X) has
generators

    [t,x]       (t in S_L^1, x in X)

and relations, for u=L_x(y), v=R_y(x),

    [L_v t,u] = [t,y],
    [t,v] = [L_y t,x] triangleright [t,y].

Finite product-Hurwitz labels are exactly finite rack quotients of U_L(X), and
similarly on the right for U_R(X).  The missing statement is finite orbit
separation by such a quotient.

3. There is a sharper prefix-path rack vertexization theorem.

Let S=S_L^1 and define prefix products

    p_0=1,
    p_i=L_{x_1}...L_{x_i}.

Use the rack convention

    r_Y(a,b)=(b,b triangleright a).

Suppose there are a finite rack Y, a map

    psi:S x X -> Y,

and inert observers iota_n:X^n -> I_n such that, for every p,x,y and
r_X(x,y)=(u,v),

    psi(p,u) = psi(pL_x,y),                                  (V1)
    psi(pL_u,v) = psi(pL_x,y) triangleright psi(p,x).        (V2)

Then

    Psi_n(x_1,...,x_n)=
      (psi(p_0,x_1),...,psi(p_{n-1},x_n))

is B_n-equivariant.  If (Psi_n,iota_n) is injective for every n, then

    ker rho_n^Y <= ker rho_n^X

for every n.

This theorem converts a finite prefix-path vertexization into Sawin
domination.

The proposed reduced branch is now:

    If a finite solution has unavoidable product observers, then either
      it has a proper kernel-reflecting active factor,
    or
      its prefix-path groupoid admits a finite rack vertexization.

A kernel-reflecting active factor means a proper factor Z, quotient-like or
coordinate-active, with

    ker rho_n^Z <= ker rho_n^X

or equality, for every n, so rack domination of Z implies rack domination of X.
Ordinary equivariant quotients are not enough because their kernel containment
usually points the wrong way.
```
