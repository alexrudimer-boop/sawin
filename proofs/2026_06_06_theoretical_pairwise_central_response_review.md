# Review: Pairwise-Or-Central Response

Date: 2026-06-06

Verdict: C.  The response does not prove Sawin's statement and does not give
an explicit cofinal counterexample.  It gives proof-grade progress by
compressing the chief-factor obstruction to either a two-meridian obstruction
or a central elementary abelian high-Brunnian obstruction.

## Theorem/Proof Progress

The pairwise-or-central compression theorem is valid under the stated
generation hypothesis.  Let `G` be finite and generated by normal subgroups
`N_1,...,N_r`, let `K` be normal, and set

```text
C=[N_1,...,N_r]_Sigma.
```

If `K cap C != 1`, the chief-factor theorem supplies a chief factor `A/B`
inside `K` covered by `C`.  Since `C<=N_i` for every `i`, every `N_i` covers
`A/B`.

For a nonabelian chief factor `V=A/B`, this immediately gives pairwise
coverage: `V` is perfect, and `V<=N_iB/B` and `V<=N_jB/B` imply

```text
V=[V,V] <= [N_iB/B,N_jB/B].
```

Thus every pair `[N_i,N_j]` covers `A/B`.

For an abelian noncentral chief factor, `V` is an irreducible elementary
abelian `G`-module.  Since the `N_i` generate `G`, some `N_j` acts
nontrivially on `V`.  Normality of `N_j` makes `[V,N_jB/B]` a nonzero
`G`-submodule, hence all of `V`.  Since every other `N_i` covers `V`,
`[N_i,N_j]` covers the factor for every `i!=j`.

The only remaining case is central elementary abelian.  This is the only case
that can require the full all-meridian symmetric commutator rather than a
pairwise commutator.

The application to the finite-image boundary is valid for `n>=3`, because the
normal closures of the images of the last-strand free generators generate
`Gamma_n`.  Thus every finite-image obstruction is either:

```text
K_n cap [N_{i,n},N_{j,n}] != 1
```

for some pair, or a central elementary abelian chief factor inside `K_n`
covered by the all-meridian commutator.

The bounded relative lower-central depth criterion is valid when the detector
used in the Brunnian finite-image reduction contains a dominating rack for
the quotient `Z`, for example `Q=Y_Z^0 x T_2`.  If the relative kernel

```text
E_n = ker(rho^X_n(F_{n-1}) -> rho^Z_n(F_{n-1}))
```

meets `gamma_{n-1}(rho^X_n(F_{n-1}))` trivially in all sufficiently large
arities, then the all-meridian detector-kernel obstruction is trivial in
large arity.  Fixed-arity cofinality handles the remaining small arities.

## Finite Evidence

None.  This response is purely theoretical.

## Heuristic Value

The result gives a useful separation of mechanisms.  Nonabelian and
noncentral abelian obstructions are low-width in the meridian normal closures.
Only central elementary abelian chief factors can represent genuinely
high-Brunnian finite-image behavior.

## Unsupported Claims

The response does not prove that every finite bijective solution admits a
finite detector with no pairwise obstruction and no central high-Brunnian
obstruction.

The response does not construct an explicit finite solution with a cofinal
sequence of pairwise or central elementary abelian obstructions.

The bounded relative lower-central depth criterion is conditional; no proof is
given that every finite solution has a dominated quotient `Z` satisfying the
relative-depth condition.

No bounded-arity computation is promoted to an all-arity theorem.
