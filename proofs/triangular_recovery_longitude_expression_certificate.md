# Triangular recovery longitude expression certificate

Date: 2026-05-31

This note follows `proofs/triangular_recovery_unit_observer.md` and
`proofs/triangular_recovery_detector_lift_fork.md`.  It makes the explicit
endpoint-longitude expression route for the fixed triangular recovery group
`U_tri` executable.  It is a supplied-certificate route; it does not construct
the missing all-`n` expressions by itself.

## Executable object

The helper

```text
triangular_recovery_longitude_expression_audit(
    interval,
    n,
    braid_word,
    factor_row_indices,
    assignment,
    expression,
)
```

specializes

```text
unit_composite_longitude_expression_audit(...)
```

to the finite group generated by the triangular recovery row permutations.
The endpoint word is the product of the recovery-row generators indexed by
`factor_row_indices`.  The supplied `assignment` gives a homomorphism from the
free meridians to `U_tri`, and `expression` is a word in the evaluated
recursive Artin longitudes for that assignment.

The audit records:

```text
factors_are_triangular_recovery_generators
endpoint_lies_in_recovery_unit_longitude_subgroup_by_expression
identity_longitudes_kill_recovery_endpoint_by_expression
proves_recovery_endpoint_by_longitude_expression
```

## Lemma: expression certificates prove the U_tri endpoint route

[Proved, supplied-data form] If the supplied expression evaluates to the
recovery endpoint composite in `U_tri`, then that endpoint lies in

```text
V_beta(U_tri).
```

Proof.  The generic unit-composite expression audit verifies that:

1. every recovery factor is in the fixed transformation monoid;
2. the endpoint composite is a unit of `U_tri`;
3. the supplied meridian assignment lies in `U_tri`;
4. the word in evaluated recursive longitudes equals the endpoint composite.

By definition, `V_beta(U_tri)` is generated by all evaluated recursive
longitudes over all assignments into `U_tri`.  Therefore a product of such
values lies in `V_beta(U_tri)`.  QED.

## Consequence under identity longitude data

[Proved] If the expression certificate passes and the finite `U_tri`
Artin-longitude signature of `beta` is the identity signature, then the
recovery endpoint composite is the identity permutation of the tagged recovery
universe.

Proof.  Under the identity signature, every evaluated recursive longitude in
`U_tri` is the group identity, so `V_beta(U_tri)={1}`.  The endpoint lies in
that subgroup by the expression certificate, hence it is identity.  QED.

## Relation to the detector-lift and derived-series forks

The detector-lift route constructs such expressions uniformly by carrying
active `(meridian, endpoint)` labels.  The derived-series route decomposes the
same endpoint expression problem through abelian quotients and the stable
perfect residual.  This note fills the direct supplied-expression slot between
those two strategies:

```text
detector-lift row theorem
  => explicit terminal longitude expressions
  => triangular_recovery_longitude_expression_audit(...)
  => endpoint in V_beta(U_tri).
```
