# Rack Full-Twist Order Bound

This note records a rack-specific tower invariant suggested by the central
full twist.  It is a genuine all-arity rack theorem, not a finite-prefix
search certificate.

Let `Y` be a finite rack in the left convention

```text
r(a,b) = (a*b, a).
```

Write `L_y` for left translation by `y`, and let `Inn(Y)` be the finite group
generated by the `L_y`.  On `n` strands use the positive central full twist

```text
Delta_n^2 = (sigma_1 sigma_2 ... sigma_{n-1})^n.
```

For a tuple `y=(y_1,...,y_n)`, set

```text
P(y) = L_{y_1} L_{y_2} ... L_{y_n} in Inn(Y).
```

The Artin-longitude formula for `Delta_n^2` gives, for every coordinate `i`,

```text
(Delta_n^2 y)_i = P(y) L_{y_i}^{-1}(y_i).
```

For the last coordinate this reads
`L_{y_1} ... L_{y_{n-1}}(y_n)`, since the factor `L_{y_n}^{-1}` cancels the
last entry of `P(y)`.

The rack identity implies the conjugation rule

```text
L_{h(y)} = h L_y h^{-1}  for every h in Inn(Y).
```

Therefore, after one full twist,

```text
L_{(Delta_n^2 y)_i} = P(y) L_{y_i} P(y)^{-1}
```

for every `i`, and the total product is preserved:

```text
P(Delta_n^2 y) = P(y).
```

Iterating, let `y_i(k)` be the `i`-th coordinate after `k` full twists and
put `z_i(k)=P(y)^{-k} y_i(k)`.  Then

```text
z_i(k+1) = L_{y_i}^{-1} z_i(k).
```

If `e=exp Inn(Y)`, then `P(y)^e=1` and `L_{y_i}^e=1`, so `z_i(e)=z_i(0)` and
`y_i(e)=y_i(0)` for every tuple and coordinate.  Hence

```text
(Delta_n^2)^e acts trivially on Y^n for every n.
```

Thus the order of the full twist action on `Y^n` divides `exp Inn(Y)`,
uniformly in `n`.

## Consequence for Domination

If a finite rack `Y` dominates a finite bijective YBE solution `X`, then for
every `n` there is a marked quotient from the braid image on `Y^n` to the
braid image on `X^n`.  Consequently the order of
`rho_X,n(Delta_n^2)` must divide the order of `rho_Y,n(Delta_n^2)`, and hence
is bounded independently of `n` by `exp Inn(Y)`.

So an explicit finite bijective YBE solution `X` with unbounded orders

```text
ord rho_X,n(Delta_n^2)
```

would rule out finite rack domination.  The current small exhaustive checks
for degenerate solutions of sizes two and three do not produce such growth:
their full-twist actions are trivial in the degenerate cases.  The remaining
obstruction task is therefore precise:

```text
Either construct a finite degenerate bijective YBE solution whose full-twist
orders grow with n, or prove that finite bijective YBE solutions also have a
uniform full-twist order bound by another finite invariant.
```

## Executable Audit

The helper `rack_full_twist_order_bound_audit(Y,n)` checks the fixed-degree
identities used above:

- the action order of `(sigma_1 ... sigma_{n-1})^n` on `Y^n`;
- the exponent of `Inn(Y)`;
- preservation of the total translation product `P(y)`;
- conjugation of each coordinate translation by `P(y)`;
- divisibility of the full-twist action order by `exp Inn(Y)`.

This audit is a fixed-`n` regression for the symbolic argument above.  It does
not by itself certify any unbounded family for non-rack YBE solutions.
