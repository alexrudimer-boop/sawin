# Point-Pushing Centralizer-Stem Transport Split

Date: 2026-05-30

This note refines the noncyclic quotient from
`proofs/point_pushing_centralizer_stem_noncyclic_quotient.md`.

It does not prove outcome A or B.  It separates centralizer-stem multiplier
tails according to whether the bounded newest generator normally generates
the quotient internally, or whether ambient transport is genuinely needed.

## Setup

Keep the centralizer-stem notation:

```text
N = <<t>>_H,
M <= Z(N) cap [N,N],
Q = N/M.
```

Let `q` be the image of `t` in `Q`.  The quotient `Q` is finite and noncyclic.
It is generated by the ambient `H`-transported conjugates of `q`, because
`N=<<t>>_H`.

Let

```text
L = <<q>>_Q
```

be the ordinary normal closure of `q` inside `Q`.

## Theorem

Exactly one of the following holds.

1. **Internal bounded normal-generator quotient.**

   ```text
   L = Q.
   ```

   Then `Q` itself is normally generated by one element whose order divides
   `B_X`.

2. **Transport-orbit generated quotient.**

   ```text
   L < Q.
   ```

   Then the quotient is not generated by the internal normal closure of the
   newest generator image.  Its generation must use the ambient transported
   orbit of that normal closure:

   ```text
   Q = < h L h^{-1} : h in H >
   ```

   after passing to the induced action on `Q`.

## Proof

The subgroup `L=<<q>>_Q` is normal in `Q` by definition.  Either `L=Q` or
`L<Q`.

If `L=Q`, then `Q` is internally normally generated by `q`.  The order of
`q` divides `ord(t)`, and the bounded normal-generator theorem gives
`ord(t)|B_X`.

If `L<Q`, then internal normal generation fails.  However, `N=<<t>>_H`, so
the images in `Q=N/M` of all ambient `H`-conjugates of `t` generate `Q`.
Equivalently, the transported conjugates of `L` under the induced ambient
action generate `Q`.  Thus the row is a genuine transport-orbit generated
quotient.  QED.

## Consequence For The Fork

The noncyclic centralizer-stem multiplier branch splits into two sharper
targets:

1. rule out noncyclic finite quotients `Q` that are internally normally
   generated by a bounded-order element and carry the required stem multiplier
   quotient; or
2. rule out quotients where the internal normal closure is proper and the
   missing generation comes from ambient transport of that closure.

The second case is the one visible in the `Q8/Z(Q8)` finite audit row: one
order-two image gives only one line in `C_2 x C_2`, and the remaining line is
created by the ambient `C_3` transport.

The follow-up `proofs/point_pushing_internal_stem_abelianization_bound.md`
sharpens the first case: if the bounded generator image internally normally
generates `Q`, then `Q_ab` is cyclic and generated by that image, hence has
order and exponent dividing `B_X`.
