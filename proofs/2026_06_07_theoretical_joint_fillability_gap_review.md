# Review: Joint-Fillability Gap

Date: 2026-06-07

Verdict: no A/B.

This response did not prove Sawin's finite-rack domination statement and did
not construct a fixed finite counterexample.  It gave concrete local evidence
against the hope that all virtual contextual states in the two-sided
contextual construction can be eliminated by realization.

## Claimed Four-Point Obstruction

Use the checked four-point solution from the fiber-monodromy review, with
elements ordered

```text
00, 01, 10, 11.
```

The response identifies maps in the contextual left/right monoids

```text
alpha=(01,01,10,11),
beta =(00,00,10,11),
```

where the tuple records images of `(00,01,10,11)`.

In the two-sided contextual quotient generated by

```text
(A,x,B r_y) ~ (A m_u,v,B),     R(x,y)=(u,v),
```

the classes represented by

```text
p=(alpha,00,alpha),
q=(alpha,00,beta)
```

are claimed to be individually realizable contextual strand states, but not
jointly fillable.  That is, no representatives of their equivalence classes
can be placed into adjacent compatible form

```text
(A,x,B r_y),    (A m_x,y,B)
```

with common `A,B,x,y`.

## Consequence

This blocks the natural attempt to eliminate virtual contextual states by
realization even inside a finite YBE-origin example.  YBE controls products
only for jointly fillable pairs.  A finite rack completion still has to assign
values to products like `p*q`, make all left translations bijective, and
satisfy global rack identities such as

```text
L_{p*q} L_p = L_p L_q
```

for products involving non-fillable pairs, while preserving the all-arity
readout.

Thus the positive route now needs either:

```text
1. a finite completion theorem for non-fillable contextual pairs in the
   YBE-origin partial augmented rack, or
2. a proof that non-fillable products can be assigned harmlessly without
   affecting orbit separation, or
3. a counterexample showing such an assignment is impossible and using it to
   build B.
```

## Negative Route

The negative route remains blocked at fixed-target cofinality.  For fixed
finite `X`, the two-strand pure order

```text
d=ord rho^X_2(sigma_1^2)
```

is fixed, so the known powered-Brunnian construction is neutralized by rack
prefixes whose pure detector orders are divisible by `d`.  No replacement
fixed-target witness family was given.
