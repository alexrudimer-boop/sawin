# Point-Pushing Brunnian Relation Lift

Date: 2026-05-30

This note refines `proofs/point_pushing_brunnian_orbit_criterion.md`.  It
separates the one-new-strand Brunnian extension obstruction into two finite
relation-lifting failures:

1. the detector conjugacy orbit of the new generator does not have a
   well-defined transported action label;
2. the orbit labels are well-defined, but a relation among detector orbit
   generators fails in the action orbit group.

It does not prove outcome A or B.  It gives the exact relation package that a
positive extension theorem must prove uniformly, or that a negative tail must
violate for infinitely many symmetric detectors.

## Setup

Use the notation of `proofs/point_pushing_brunnian_orbit_criterion.md`.
At the one-new-strand step, write the old suffix paired subgroup as

```text
C=<E_1,...,E_k> <= D_old x P_old,
```

and the new-generator pair as

```text
e=(d,h).
```

Let

```text
C_D=pr_D(C),     C_X=pr_X(C).
```

Assume first that the old suffix step is detected, so `C` is the graph of a
marked quotient

```text
q:C_D -> C_X.
```

Define the detector conjugacy orbit

```text
O_D={c d c^{-1}: c in C_D}.
```

There is a candidate transported action label

```text
q_e(c d c^{-1}) = q(c) h q(c)^{-1}.
```

## Theorem

Under the old-suffix graph hypothesis, the one-new-strand extension holds if
and only if both conditions hold:

1. `q_e` is well-defined on `O_D`;
2. every relation among the elements of `O_D` is also a relation among their
   transported action labels.

Equivalently, `q_e` extends to a homomorphism

```text
<O_D> -> <q_e(O_D)>
```

sending each detector conjugate to its transported action conjugate.

## Proof

The Brunnian orbit criterion identifies the relative obstruction subgroup as

```text
B=<c e c^{-1}: c in C>.
```

Because `C` is the graph of `q`, the detector projection of `B` is

```text
B_D=<O_D>,
```

and the action projection is generated by the transported labels

```text
q(c) h q(c)^{-1}.
```

If `q_e` is not well-defined, then there are `c_1,c_2 in C_D` such that

```text
c_1 d c_1^{-1}=c_2 d c_2^{-1}
```

but

```text
q(c_1) h q(c_1)^{-1} != q(c_2) h q(c_2)^{-1}.
```

Choosing words `u_1,u_2 in F_k` representing `c_1,c_2`, the Brunnian word

```text
u_1 a_{k+1} u_1^{-1}
(u_2 a_{k+1} u_2^{-1})^{-1}
```

has trivial detector value and nontrivial action value.  Thus it is already a
one-new-strand vertical witness.

Assume now that `q_e` is well-defined.  The paired subgroup `B` is the graph
of a homomorphism from `B_D=<O_D>` to the action orbit group exactly when every
word in the orbit generators that evaluates to `1` in `B_D` also evaluates to
`1` after applying `q_e`.  By the paired graph criterion, this graph condition
is exactly triviality of the relative vertical kernel:

```text
B cap ({1} x P_{k+1}(X))={(1,1)}.
```

By the Brunnian orbit criterion, that is exactly the absence of a
one-new-strand Brunnian witness.  QED.

## A-Side Extension Target

For a fixed detector `S_m`, the all-arity positive extension target is now:

```text
for every k,
the transported new-generator orbit map q_e is well-defined
and all detector-orbit relations lift.
```

If this holds for one `m=m(X)`, then the induction from
`proofs/point_pushing_jump_normalization.md` gives

```text
R_{m,k} <= N_{X,k}
```

for every `k`, hence the sharp rack `A_{S_m}` dominates `X`.

## B-Side Tail Target

A B tail can now be forced into one of two explicit finite forms for every
`j`:

1. a transported-label ambiguity in the detector conjugacy orbit of the new
   generator for `S_j`;
2. a relation among detector orbit generators that fails among transported
   action labels.

Either form gives a right-based Brunnian word in `K_{S_j}` moving `X`, and
right stabilization gives the normalized-law obstruction sequence.

## Audit Hook

The helper

```text
point_pushing_brunnian_orbit_audit(...)
```

now records:

- `detector_orbit_size`;
- `action_orbit_size`;
- `orbit_map_well_defined`;
- a right-based witness word if either transported-label ambiguity or a later
  orbit-relation failure produces a vertical element.

It is still a finite-row certificate checker, not a proof of uniform
boundedness.
