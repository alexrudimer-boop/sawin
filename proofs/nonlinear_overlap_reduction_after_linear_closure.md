# Nonlinear overlap reduction after linear closure

Date: 2026-05-31

This note records what remains of the prompt's nonlinear overlap obstruction
after the finite-linear system is closed in
`proofs/finite_linear_overlap_resolution.md`.

It is not a final A or B proof.  It proves that any surviving nonlinear
overlap obstruction must be exactly a local descent-endpoint obstruction in
the `bi_free_universal_corridor_bottleneck` branch, and it records the
precise extra condition needed to turn such an obstruction into B.

## Localization of any nonlinear survivor

[Proved] Any remaining nonlinear overlap obstruction must occur in a
local-minimal `bi_free_universal_corridor_bottleneck` interval.

Proof.  By the congruence-chain reduction, a global counterexample can be
chosen in a local-minimal quotient interval.  The known closed branches listed
above remove the rack, nondegenerate, involutive/permutation, product,
coboundary, affine-linear finite-search-only, operator-Alexander, non-overlap,
and finite-linear primitive overlap cases.  If an interval is not
local-minimal, it is refined along an intermediate admissible congruence and
is not a terminal obstruction.  Genuine affine or nonlinear endpoint/gauge
cocycles that are not coboundaries and do not factor through a fixed finite
extension are not removed by the affine audit tags; they are part of the
same endpoint/gauge burden inside the branch left after those routers:
`bi_free_universal_corridor_bottleneck`.  QED.

## Continuation form of the nonlinear branch

[Proved] In such a local-minimal nonlinear survivor, either the lower row is
strand-continuing and already rack-controlled, or every nontrivial
continuation seed individually generates the all-universal admissible
congruence.

Proof.  Work in the rack-base convention

```text
R_A(a,b)=(a*b,a).
```

For a local row

```text
T_{a,b}(x,y)=(u,v),
```

the continuation seed is `x~v`.  The least admissible congruence generated by
all such seeds is `Theta^cont`.

If `Theta^cont` is equality, every row is strand-continuing.  Then
`proofs/transport_state_rackification_detector.md` applies: after adjoining
the finite lower transport state, the row is a finite rack row, detected by
the fixed group `Inn(A x E)`.

If `Theta^cont` is non-equality in a local-minimal interval, local-minimality
forces it to be all-universal.  Moreover
`proofs/elementary_continuation_closure.md` proves the single-seed version:
each individual nontrivial continuation seed has universal closure.  QED.

Thus nonlinear overlap is no longer an unspecified "middle carrier" effect.
It is an elementary universal-continuation seed together with its finite
derivation ledger from
`proofs/universal_continuation_derivation_certificate.md`.

## Endpoint/readout form of the remaining obstruction

[Proved] If the descent-endpoint repair contract holds for every such
interval, then no nonlinear overlap obstruction remains.

Proof.  The repair contract supplies:

```text
1. a fixed descent-separating readout;
2. fixed endpoint groups;
3. faithful reconstruction from the readout plus endpoint channels;
4. all-n V_beta witnesses for every endpoint channel.
```

The readout quotient is strand-continuing, so the transport-state
rackification detector kills the quotient transport motion.  The endpoint
witnesses lie in fixed finite groups and assemble into one product detector by
the direct-product longitude lemma.  The faithful reconstruction rule then
forces the original residual action to be trivial whenever the product
detector has identity recursive-longitude data.  This is exactly the local
finite detector implication required by the sharp obstruction theorem.  QED.

Consequently a genuine nonlinear survivor must fail this fixed
descent-endpoint package: some universal continuation seed or derived edge is
not killed by any supplied admissible fixed readout with routed endpoint
`V_beta` witnesses and faithful reconstruction.

## What is equivalent to B

[Proved] A finite failure of a proposed readout package is not yet B.  B is
equivalent to the stronger statement that the resulting local interval has no
finite group detector at all.

Proof.  The local normalized-law fork in
`proofs/global_local_normalized_fork.md` and
`proofs/normalized_law_domination_dichotomy.md` proves the exact dichotomy for
a fixed local interval over a rack-dominated quotient:

```text
either there is a finite group G(pi,Q), independent of n, such that
identity G-longitude data kills every residual action;

or there is a normalized-law residual obstruction sequence invisible to every
finite group and moving a residual tuple.
```

The first alternative gives A for that interval by the sharp obstruction
theorem.  The second alternative defeats every finite rack, because every
finite rack action is killed by the corresponding finite inner-group
longitude data.  QED.

Thus the nonlinear B target is exact:

```text
construct one explicit finite local-minimal bi-free universal-corridor
interval, prove it has no finite group detector, and use the normalized-law
fork to obtain the braid sequence.
```

Equivalently, an A proof must show that every nonlinear survivor has a fixed
finite detector; the repair contract is the current concrete certificate
format for such a detector.

## Final narrowed obstruction

[Open] The only remaining obstruction after finite-linear closure is:

```text
An explicit local-minimal bi-free universal-corridor interval with an
elementary universal-continuation seed and terminal endpoint/gauge motion
such that either the kink-completion deficit cannot be routed to a fixed
detector, or no fixed finite group G(pi,Q) kills the residual action by

```

To prove B, the interval must be accompanied by the normalized-law braid

for every such interval.
