# Non-Powered Global Brunnian Rack Laws

This note records the post-powered-meridian boundary.  The powered-meridian
normal subgroup cannot be frozen to one fixed target, but that does not mean
bounded rack prefixes have no high-arity Brunnian laws.  They do: the laws can
be genuinely global rather than consequences of individual meridians becoming
trivial.

## Theorem

Let `U` be a finite rack, and put

```text
e=ord(rho^U_2(sigma_1^2)).
```

Assume `e>=2`.  For every

```text
r>=2,        n=r+1,
```

there exists a nontrivial Brunnian braid

```text
beta_r in Brun_n
```

such that

```text
rho^U_n(beta_r)=1,
```

but

```text
beta_r notin << A_{1,n}^e,...,A_{r,n}^e >> <= F_r,
```

where

```text
F_r=ker(d_n:P_n->P_{n-1})
    =<x_1,...,x_r>,
x_i=A_{i,n}.
```

Thus `beta_r` is invisible to `U`, Brunnian, and not produced by the normal
subgroup generated by individually powered meridians.

## Proof

Let

```text
R=ker(F_r->rho^U_n(F_r))
```

be the kernel of the `U`-action on the last-strand free group.  Each meridian
`x_i=A_{i,n}` is conjugate in the braid group to a two-strand pure square, so
the order of `rho^U_n(x_i)` divides `e`.  Hence

```text
x_i^e in R        for every i.
```

Let

```text
P_e=<<x_1^e,...,x_r^e>>_{F_r}.
```

Then

```text
P_e <= R.
```

Now quotient:

```text
bar F_r=F_r/P_e ~= C_e * C_e * ... * C_e
```

with `r` free-product factors.  Let `bar x_i` be the image of `x_i`.

Consider the left-normed commutator

```text
c_r=[...[ [x_1,x_2],x_3],...,x_r] in F_r.
```

It is Brunnian: deleting any generator `x_i` makes one input of the iterated
commutator equal to `1`, and hence the whole word becomes `1`.

Its image

```text
bar c_r in bar F_r
```

is nontrivial and has infinite order.  This follows from the normal form
theorem for free products: the reduced word for the iterated commutator is
cyclically reduced and not conjugate into any finite factor.

The finite `U`-image gives a finite quotient

```text
bar F_r -> F_r/R.
```

Since `bar c_r` has infinite order but `F_r/R` is finite, some positive power

```text
bar c_r^t
```

lies in the kernel of this quotient while remaining nontrivial in `bar F_r`.
Lift back to

```text
beta_r=c_r^t in F_r.
```

Then `beta_r in R`, so

```text
rho^U_n(beta_r)=1.
```

Also

```text
beta_r notin P_e,
```

because its image `bar c_r^t` is nontrivial in `bar F_r`.

Finally, `c_r` is Brunnian and the Brunnian subgroup is a subgroup, so
`c_r^t` is Brunnian:

```text
beta_r in Brun_{r+1}.
```

This proves the theorem.

## Consequence For Rack Prefixes

Let

```text
U_M=prod_{|Y|<=M}Y
```

be the product of all finite racks of size at most `M`, and assume

```text
e_M=ord(rho^{U_M}_2(sigma_1^2))>=2.
```

Then for every `r>=2` there is

```text
beta_r in Brun_{r+1}
```

with

```text
rho^{U_M}_{r+1}(beta_r)=1,
```

and `beta_r in F_r <= P_{r+1}`, so it is pure and `T_2`-invisible.  Thus no
rack of size at most `M` detects `beta_r`, and therefore

```text
r(beta_r)>M.
```

But

```text
beta_r notin << A_{1,n}^{e_M},...,A_{r,n}^{e_M} >>.
```

Thus unbounded finite-rack residual complexity persists even after removing
the powered-meridian normal subgroup.

## Updated Boundary

The powered-meridian no-freezing theorem says:

```text
powered-meridian Brunnian ghosts cannot yield a fixed-target counterexample.
```

This theorem says the negative route is still alive in principle:

```text
global Brunnian rack laws exist for every bounded rack prefix.
```

A fixed-target counterexample must make one fixed finite `X` see such
non-powered global Brunnian rack laws in one fixed contextual transition
cofinally.

Concretely, a negative solution must construct one fixed finite `X`, one
dominated congruence `E`, one fixed contextual transition

```text
p_* -> p'_*,
```

and a sequence

```text
beta_m in Brun_{n_m},        n_m->infinity,
```

such that

```text
rho^{P_m^0 x T_2}_{n_m}(beta_m)=1,
rho^{X/E}_{n_m}(beta_m)=1,
rho^X_{n_m}(beta_m) realizes p_* -> p'_*,
```

and

```text
beta_m notin << A_{i,n_m}^{e_m} >>.
```

A positive solution must prove that no fixed finite `X` can see these
non-powered global Brunnian rack laws in one fixed contextual transition
cofinally.
