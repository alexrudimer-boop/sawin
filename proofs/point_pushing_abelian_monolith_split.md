# Point-Pushing Abelian Monolith Split

Date: 2026-05-30

This note refines `proofs/point_pushing_monolith_type_split.md` in the
elementary-abelian monolith case.  It does not prove outcome A or B.  It
prevents a false simplification: an abelian monolith is not automatically just
a visible linear quotient.  There are two cases, a central-extension case and
a noncentral irreducible-module case.

## Setup

Let a product-prefix first failure have already been compressed as in
`proofs/point_pushing_monolithic_compression.md`:

```text
w in ker(F_k -> F_{k-1}),
w=1 in D_k(Pi_j),
phi(w(h_1,...,h_k)) = m in M \ {1},
```

where

```text
phi:P_k(X)->H
```

is a smallest separating quotient, `H` is finite monolithic, and `M` is its
unique minimal nontrivial normal subgroup.  Assume now that

```text
M ~= (C_p)^r
```

is elementary abelian.

## Theorem

Exactly one of the following holds.

1. **Central abelian monolith.**

   ```text
   [H,M]=1.
   ```

   Then `M <= Z(H)`, the moved value `m` is a central monolith element, and
   the obstruction is a central-extension or Frattini-depth obstruction.  No
   purely conjugation-linear quotient can see `m`.

2. **Noncentral abelian monolith.**

   ```text
   [H,M]=M.
   ```

   Then `M` is an irreducible `F_p[H]`-module under conjugation,

   ```text
   H/C_H(M) <= GL(M)
   ```

   acts faithfully and irreducibly on `M`, and the moved value `m` lies in the
   subgroup generated by conjugation differences

   ```text
   h a h^-1 a^-1        (h in H, a in M).
   ```

   Thus the obstruction has a genuine irreducible linear-module shadow.

## Proof

Because `M` is abelian of exponent `p`, we may write it additively as a finite
`F_p`-vector space.  Conjugation by `H` gives an `F_p`-linear action on `M`.

If `0 != V < M` is an `H`-invariant subgroup, then `V` is normal in `H`.
This contradicts the minimality of the monolith `M`.  Hence `M` is irreducible
as an `F_p[H]`-module.

Now consider the commutator subgroup

```text
[H,M] = < h a h^-1 a^-1 : h in H, a in M >.
```

It is contained in `M`, because `M` is normal.  It is also `H`-invariant, hence
normal in `H`.  Since `M` is a minimal nontrivial normal subgroup, either

```text
[H,M]=1
```

or

```text
[H,M]=M.
```

In the first case every element of `H` centralizes every element of `M`, so
`M <= Z(H)`.  The distinguished moved element `m` is central.  Its
nontriviality cannot be read from the conjugation action, because that action
is trivial on `M`.

In the second case, the conjugation action is nontrivial and `[H,M]=M`.
The kernel of the conjugation action on `M` is the centralizer `C_H(M)`, so
`H/C_H(M)` embeds faithfully in `GL(M)`.  Irreducibility was proved above.
Since `[H,M]=M`, every element of `M`, in particular the moved value `m`, is a
product of conjugation differences.  QED.

## Consequence For B

After monolithic compression and the type split, an abelian-monolith negative
tail has a further dichotomy:

```text
central abelian monolith tail,
noncentral irreducible-module tail.
```

A B proof in the central tail must produce unbounded central monolithic
quotients whose distinguished bottom element remains product-prefix invisible.
The follow-up note `proofs/point_pushing_central_monolith_depth.md` proves
that such a tail is either cyclic `p`-power bottom-layer depth or a central
stem-extension tail with the monolith in `Z(H) cap [H,H]`.

A B proof in the noncentral tail must produce unbounded irreducible
conjugation-module shadows, possibly together with additional centralizer
layers over the monolith.  The follow-up note
`proofs/point_pushing_noncentral_module_tail.md` proves that such a tail has
an infinite subsequence with module prime escape, fixed-prime module-dimension
escape, or bounded-module centralizer-layer escape.

Thus the remaining abelian regime is no longer a single black box.  A positive
proof may close it by proving uniform bounds for both:

1. central monolithic depth of point-pushing action images; and
2. noncentral irreducible `F_p` conjugation-module quotients.

## Audit Hook

The helper

```text
point_pushing_monolithic_compression_audit(...)
```

now records the finite-row conjugation data of the compressed monolith:

```text
monolith_centralizer_order,
monolith_action_quotient_order,
monolith_commutator_order,
monolith_is_central.
```

For an elementary-abelian monolith, `monolith_commutator_order` is forced to be
either `1` or `monolith_order` by the theorem above.  The helper is a finite
row check only; it does not supply the required all-`k` uniform bound.
