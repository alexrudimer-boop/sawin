# Point-Pushing Abelian Centralizer-Layer Prime Bound

Date: 2026-05-30

This note refines the abelian side of
`proofs/point_pushing_centralizer_layer_commutator_split.md`.

It does not prove outcome A or B.  It removes prime escape from abelian
centralizer-layer generator tails.

## Setup

Work in a minimal monolithic quotient `H` of a product-prefix first-failure
Brunnian row.  Let the abelian monolith be

```text
M ~= F_p^r.
```

Let `t` be the image of the newest point-pushing generator.  The bounded
normal-generator theorem gives

```text
ord(t) | B_X,
M <= <<t>>_H.
```

Assume we are in the abelian centralizer-layer branch.  Thus

```text
N = <<t>>_H,
N <= C_H(M),
[N,N]=1,
M <= N.
```

## Theorem

The subgroup `N` is a finite abelian `p`-group.  Moreover,

```text
exp(N) | ord(t) | B_X,
```

and therefore

```text
p | ord(t) | B_X.
```

Consequently an infinite abelian centralizer-layer tail cannot have module
prime escape.  Its prime belongs to the finite set of prime divisors of the
fixed integer `B_X`, and its abelian centralizer-layer exponent is bounded by
`B_X`.

## Proof

Since `N` is finite abelian, it decomposes as the product of its primary
components.  For each prime `q`, write `N_q` for the subgroup of all elements
of `q`-power order.  This subgroup is characteristic in `N`.

The subgroup `N` is normal in `H`, so each nontrivial `N_q` is normal in `H`.
The quotient `H` is monolithic with unique minimal nontrivial normal subgroup
`M`.  Therefore every nontrivial normal subgroup of `H` contains `M`.

If `q != p` and `N_q` were nontrivial, then `M <= N_q`.  This is impossible
because `M` is a `p`-group while `N_q` is a `q`-group.  Hence `N_q=1` for
every `q != p`, so `N` is a `p`-group.

The subgroup `N` is generated by the `H`-conjugates of `t`.  Because `N` is
abelian, every generator has the same order as `t`, and the exponent of the
generated abelian group divides `ord(t)`.  Hence

```text
exp(N) | ord(t).
```

The bounded normal-generator theorem gives `ord(t)|B_X`, so `exp(N)|B_X`.
Since `M` is nontrivial and `M<=N`, the `p`-group `N` is nontrivial and
`p|exp(N)`.  Thus `p|ord(t)|B_X`.  QED.

## Consequence For The Fork

The abelian centralizer-layer branch is not a new prime-escape branch.  It is
a bounded-prime, bounded-exponent `p`-primary extension layer.

The remaining abelian centralizer-layer obstruction, if it exists, must be
unbounded in rank or extension structure at one of the finitely many primes
dividing `B_X`; it cannot be caused by primes escaping beyond the fixed
point-pushing generator order bound.

## Audit Hook

The helper

```text
point_pushing_abelian_centralizer_layer_prime_audit(...)
```

forms `N=<<t>>`, computes its exponent and prime support, and records whether
the finite row is a

```text
bounded_p_primary_abelian_centralizer_layer.
```

Rows with mixed prime support are labeled

```text
mixed_prime_or_unbounded_generator_layer
```

and cannot be final minimal abelian centralizer-layer rows under the theorem
above.
