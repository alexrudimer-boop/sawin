# Review Of The Ordinary-Brunnian Theoretical Response

Date: 2026-06-06

Verdict: C.  The response does not prove Sawin's statement and does not give
a cofinal counterexample.  It gives proof-grade progress: the fully
deletion-minimal `X`-Brunnian obstruction can be normalized to an ordinary
Brunnian braid, and a uniformly nilpotent last-strand-image class is
Sawin-positive.

## Theorem/Proof Progress

1.  The ordinary-Brunnian sharpening is valid, with the induction invariant
    made explicit.  Starting from a fully deletion-minimal witness, one kills
    one-strand deletions by multiplying by inserted copies of those deletions.
    The proof uses the face-section identities for pure braid deletion and
    trivial-strand insertion.

2.  A technical caveat is important: `ker rho^X_n` is not generally
    deletion-closed for an arbitrary YBE solution.  The normalization does not
    require such a false closure property.  It only uses the maintained
    invariant that all proper deletions of the current witness lie in
    `K_m=ker rho^Q_m cap ker rho^X_m`, and insertion preserves `K`.

3.  The cofinal negative target is sharpened.  If no finite rack dominates
    `X`, then for every finite rack prefix `P_m`, with
    `Q_m=P_m^0 x T_2`, there are ordinary Brunnian braids

    ```text
    beta_m in Brun_{n_m} cap ker rho^{Q_m}_{n_m}
    ```

    that are `X`-visible.  The arities `n_m` go to infinity by fixed-arity
    rack cofinality.

4.  The uniformly nilpotent last-strand-image theorem is valid with one
    correction: the finite rack dominator is

    ```text
    Q = Y_0^0 x T_2,
    ```

    not necessarily the unextended product `Y_0`.

5.  The proof of the nilpotent criterion uses two standard facts:
    `F_{n-1}=ker(d_n:P_n->P_{n-1})` is the free group generated by
    `A_{1n},...,A_{n-1,n}`, and the Brunnian subgroup of a free group
    `F_r=<x_1,...,x_r>` lies in `gamma_r(F_r)`.

## Finite Evidence

No new finite evidence was supplied.  The result is theoretical.

## Heuristic Content

The remaining obstruction is now best described as unbounded ordinary
Brunnian visibility inside the last-strand free groups, not merely an
`X`-relative deletion-minimal phenomenon.

## Unsupported Claims

The response correctly avoids A and B.  The following remain unsupported:

1.  Every finite degenerate solution has uniformly nilpotent last-strand pure
    images.
2.  Every finite degenerate solution annihilates ordinary Brunnian elements
    after adding one finite rack detector.
3.  There exists an explicit finite degenerate solution with cofinal
    rack-prefix-invisible, ordinary-Brunnian, `X`-visible witnesses.
4.  The finite low-arity evidence proves the all-arity ordinary-Brunnian
    annihilation statement.
