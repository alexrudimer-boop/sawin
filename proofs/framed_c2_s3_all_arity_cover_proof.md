# Framed C2 is dominated by S3 in all arities

This note upgrades the finite arity probe

```text
proofs/framed_c2_s3_arity3_cover_probe.md
```

to an all-arity proof for the smallest framed Artin detector.

Let `C2` be the additive group `F_2`, and let

```text
A_C2 = artin_detector_rack(C2, include_trivial_two=False).
```

In the conventions of `src/ybe_domination/artin_longitudes.py`, the active
labels are pairs `(a,u) in C2 x C2`, and the rack row is

```text
(a,u) * (b,v) = (b, a+v).
```

Thus the braid generator acts by

```text
sigma_i : ((a_i,u_i),(a_{i+1},u_{i+1}))
        -> ((a_{i+1}, a_i+u_{i+1}), (a_i,u_i)).
```

The `a`-coordinates follow the ordinary strand permutation.  The `u`-coordinates
record the Artin longitude values evaluated in `C2`.

## Lemma 1: the A_C2 action factors through the mod-2 pure quotient

Let

```text
M_n = B_n / (P_n' P_n^2).
```

Then the braid action of `B_n` on `A_C2^n` factors through `M_n`.

For a pure braid beta, the `a`-coordinates are fixed.  The only possible motion
is in the `u`-coordinates.  By the Artin-longitude recursion, the change in
`u_i` is the value of the `i`-th longitude word of beta in the abelian group
`C2`, evaluated on the assigned `a`-coordinates.

Since `C2` is abelian, this value depends only on the abelianized exponent
vector of the longitude.  In the pure braid group, the abelianization is freely
generated by the standard pure generators `A_ij`, and the longitude matrix of
`A_ij` has exactly the two endpoint entries:

```text
longitude_i contains x_j once,
longitude_j contains x_i once,
all other longitude rows are zero in abelianization.
```

Therefore, for an arbitrary pure braid beta, the mod-2 longitude matrix is
exactly the pairwise linking matrix of beta modulo `2`.  Hence all pure
commutators and all pure squares act trivially on `A_C2^n`:

```text
P_n' P_n^2 <= K_n(A_C2).
```

The non-pure part of the action is just the strand permutation on the
`a`-coordinates, so the full action factors through

```text
B_n / (P_n' P_n^2) = M_n.
```

## Lemma 2: S3 detects M_n

The existing bridge

```text
proofs/mod2_pure_braid_s3_detector_bridge.md
```

proves that the conjugation rack of `S3` detects every nontrivial element of
`M_n`.  Equivalently,

```text
K_n(S3^conj) <= ker(B_n -> M_n)
```

for every arity `n`.

The argument is:

1. a nontrivial strand permutation is detected by a tuple with one nonidentity
   element of `S3` and identities elsewhere;
2. a pure braid with an odd `A_ij` coordinate is detected by assigning
   noncommuting elements to strands `i,j` and identities elsewhere, reducing to
   the two-strand action of an odd power of `sigma_1^2`.

## Theorem

For every `n >= 1`,

```text
K_n(S3^conj) <= K_n(A_C2).
```

Indeed, if beta is in `K_n(S3^conj)`, then Lemma 2 says beta maps trivially to
`M_n`.  Lemma 1 says the `A_C2` action factors through `M_n`.  Therefore beta
acts trivially on `A_C2^n`.

So `S3^conj` dominates the framed `C2` detector in all arities.

## Scope

This proves the framed-to-conjugation cover only for the rank-one framed
detector `A_C2`.

It does not prove the general Framed-to-Conjugation Cover Lemma:

```text
for every finite group G, find C(G) with
K_n(C(G)^conj) <= K_n(A_G^+)
for all n.
```

For nonabelian `G`, the framed detector records nonabelian Artin longitude
values, not merely the mod-2 pairwise-linking matrix.  The general cover lemma
therefore remains a genuine finite-group route frontier.
