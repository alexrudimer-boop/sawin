# Green section transport rigidity gate

Date: 2026-06-03

This note isolates the exact Green/corridor branch-choice rigidity question
left after the atom quotient and bounded completed-context loop guardrails.

The existing Green audit starts from a finite coordinate-action monoid

```text

```

and a regular Green `R`-class `C`.  Retained edge-germs are

```text
E_C={(s,x): s in C and s tau_x in C}.
```


```text

```

The key word is "actual": Green coordinates, Rees normal forms, or ambient
Schutzenberger permutations do not count as section transport unless they are
realized by supported completed-context morphisms.

## Rigidity Target

Let `Omega` be a recurrent hidden component that is:

- same-atom after the saturated atom quotient;
- alpha-trivial after the visible Green/Schutzenberger/atom readouts;
- depth-null in the bounded observer;
- locally group-invertible in a cyclic p-primary branch quotient

  ```text
  N_{C,d}/N_{C,d+1} ~= C_{p^m}.
  ```

Suppose two admissible first branches `e,e'` both keep the same hidden
component alive, and suppose they are comparable by an actual deterministic
completed-context transport

```text
theta_{e,e'}.
```

The positive rigidity lemma would say that the induced units agree:

```text
phi_e = phi_{e'} in Aut(C_{p^m}).
```

If true, hidden same-atom branch choices do not carry independent
section-holonomy; they route into the already fixed Green/Schutzenberger,
atom, and endpoint/unit detector product.

## Countercertificate Target

A genuine negative example must provide all of the following:

1. a finite bijective set-theoretic YBE solution;
2. a full regular Green `R`-class `C`;
3. actual supported completed-context transports, not formal Green moves;
4. a recurrent same-atom hidden section component;
5. a cyclic p-primary branch quotient `N_{C,d}/N_{C,d+1}`;
6. two comparable admissible sections `e,e'`;
7. different induced automorphisms

   ```text
   phi_e != phi_{e'} in Aut(C_{p^m}).
   ```

The raw `S_3` conjugation fibre action, a Rees sandwich coordinate, or a
Schutzenberger target normalization is not enough unless it produces such an
objectwise hidden class with actual completed-context transport.
