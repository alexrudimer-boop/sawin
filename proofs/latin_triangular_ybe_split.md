# Latin triangular YBE split

Date: 2026-05-30

This note records the next reduction after constant-column collapse.  It does
not prove the Master Local-Minimal Residual Theorem.  It shows that in the
remaining Latin-unit triangular case, the constant-coordinate transports are
not the serious obstruction: the Yang-Baxter equation makes them a
product/permutation cocycle.  The remaining triangular obstruction is the
companion shear endpoint.

## Setup

Assume the remaining lower rows are in left Latin triangular form:

```text
T_{p,q}(x,y) = (alpha_{p,q}(x), beta_{p,q,x}(y)).
```

Here

```text
x in A_p,       y in A_q,
alpha_{p,q}: A_p -> A_{p.q},
beta_{p,q,x}: A_q -> A_{p*q}.
```

The Latin-unit condition means:

- every `alpha_{p,q}` is bijective;
- every companion section `y -> beta_{p,q,x}(y)` is bijective;
- every opposite column `x -> beta_{p,q,x}(y)` is bijective.

The side-dual case has the analogous equations after reversing left and right.

## Coloured YBE expansion

Let the quotient colour row be

```text
R_Z(a,b) = (a.b, a*b).
```

For a colour triple `(a,b,c)`, write all rows in left triangular form.  Apply
the coloured YBE

```text
T12_{a.b,(a*b).c} T23_{a*b,c} T12_{a,b}
=
T23_{a*(b.c),b*c} T12_{a,b.c} T23_{b,c}.
```

Starting from `(x,y,z)`, the first coordinate equality is

```text
alpha_{a.b,(a*b).c}(alpha_{a,b}(x))
=
alpha_{a,b.c}(x).                         (A)
```

This equation is independent of `y` and `z`.

The second coordinate equality is

```text
beta_{a.b,(a*b).c, alpha_{a,b}(x)}
  (alpha_{a*b,c}(beta_{a,b,x}(y)))
=
alpha_{a*(b.c),b*c}
  (beta_{a,b.c,x}(alpha_{b,c}(y))).        (B)
```

The third coordinate equality is

```text
beta_{a*b,c, beta_{a,b,x}(y)}(z)
=
beta_{a*(b.c),b*c,
  beta_{a,b.c,x}(alpha_{b,c}(y))}
  (beta_{b,c,y}(z)).                       (C)
```

Equations `(A)`, `(B)`, and `(C)` are exactly the three coordinate projections
of the local coloured YBE in this triangular normal form.

## Consequence 1: alpha is product/permutation holonomy

Equation `(A)` says the `alpha` maps form a finite product/permutation cocycle
over the quotient-colour braid transport:

```text
alpha_{a.b,(a*b).c} o alpha_{a,b}
=
alpha_{a,b.c}.
```

Thus the alpha-only motion is not an independent Green/corridor obstruction.
It is finite coordinate holonomy through the fixed family of bijections
`alpha_{p,q}` and belongs to the already closed product/permutation route.

Equivalently, in the detector product for the interval, alpha transport should
be assigned to the known product/permutation factor, not to the final
Latin-unit shear factor.

## Consequence 2: the final triangular target is companion shear

After the alpha product/permutation factor is separated, the remaining
Latin-unit triangular theorem is:

```text
Companion-shear longitude theorem.
Every residual endpoint made from the beta companion and opposite-column
permutations lies in V_beta(U_shear),
```

where `U_shear` is the fixed finite group generated by the companion
permutations and opposite-column permutations after alpha transport is routed
to the product/permutation factor.

Equations `(B)` and `(C)` are the finite row identities that any proof of this
theorem must use.  Equation `(C)` is the endpoint shear equation; it is the
place where a genuine normalized-law B seed would have to survive.

## Executable audit

The helper

```text
latin_triangular_ybe_audit(interval)
```

collects all left Latin-unit triangular rows and, for every colour triple whose
six YBE rows are left Latin-unit triangular, records failures of:

- the alpha cocycle equation `(A)`;
- the middle companion equation `(B)`;
- the endpoint shear equation `(C)`.

For a true coloured YBE interval, these failure lists are empty.  The audit is
still useful because it exposes which part of the triangular normal form each
coordinate equation controls.  The tests include:

- a singleton triangular YBE row, where all three equations hold;
- the non-YBE Latin shear `T(x,y)=(x,x+y mod 2)`, where alpha and middle
  equations hold but endpoint shear failures are recorded.

## Updated obstruction

The remaining triangular obstruction is now sharper than

```text
Latin-unit triangular longitude.
```

It is:

```text
Companion-shear longitude visibility in U_shear.
```

A positive proof must show that residual products governed by `(B)` and `(C)`
lie in `V_beta(U_shear)` for one fixed interval-level finite group.  A negative
route must produce a Latin-unit triangular companion-shear endpoint

```text
S_beta notin V_beta(U_shear)
```

and then upgrade it to a normalized-law sequence invisible to every finite
group while still moving a residual tuple.  A failure in the alpha transport
alone is not a valid B seed, because alpha has already been routed to the
closed product/permutation holonomy branch.
