# Point-Pushing Transport Residual Abelianization Bound

Date: 2026-05-30

This note refines the abelian-visible side of
`proofs/point_pushing_transport_residual_quotient_split.md`.

It does not prove outcome A or B.  It shows that the abelian quotient left by
the transport-residual split cannot escape by new primes or unbounded exponent.

## Setup

Use the centralizer-stem transport notation:

```text
N = <<t>>_H,
M <= Z(N) cap [N,N],
Q = N/M,
q = image(t) in Q,
L = <<q>>_Q,
E = Q/L.
```

Let `B` be the fixed normal-generator order bound coming from the finite
solution `X`; in the current point-pushing row, `ord(t)` divides `B`.

In the transport-orbit branch, `L<Q`, and `Q` is generated by the ambient
transported images of `q`.  Each transported image has order dividing `B`,
because it is the image in `Q` of a conjugate/transport of `t`.

## Theorem

If the transport residual is abelian-visible, so

```text
E_ab = E/[E,E] != 1,
```

then

```text
exp(E_ab) divides B.
```

In particular, every prime dividing `|E_ab|` divides `B`.

## Proof

The quotient `E=Q/L` is generated by the images of the ambient transported
copies of `q`, because `Q` is generated by those transported copies and
quotienting by `L` only kills the internal normal closure of the original
`q`.

Each transported copy has order dividing `B` in `Q`; therefore its image in
`E` also has order dividing `B`, and its image in the abelianization `E_ab`
has order dividing `B`.

A finite abelian group generated by elements whose orders divide `B` has
exponent dividing `B`.  Hence

```text
exp(E_ab) divides B.
```

The prime-support statement follows immediately: every prime divisor of
`|E_ab|` divides `exp(E_ab)`, and `exp(E_ab)` divides `B`.  QED.

## Consequence For The Fork

The abelian-visible transport residual cannot be the source of unbounded prime
or exponent escape.

Thus a genuine transport-residual B tail must now lie in one of the following
forms:

1. an abelian-visible residual with fixed prime support and exponent dividing
   `B`, so only rank/orbit growth at the fixed primes remains; or
2. a perfect transport residual.

The remaining perfect branch is the genuinely nonabelian residual after
collapsing the internal normal closure.
