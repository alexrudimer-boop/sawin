# Point-Pushing Nonabelian Wreath Coordinate Lift

Date: 2026-05-30

This note refines the nonabelian-chief relation quotient from
`proofs/point_pushing_nonabelian_chief_relation_quotient.md`.

It does not prove outcome A or B.  It puts the nonabelian-chief obstruction in
its exact simple-wreath coordinate form.

## Setup

Fix a finite bijective YBE solution `X`, a product-prefix detector `Pi_j`, and
a first-failure one-new-strand Brunnian row at arity `k+1`.

Let

```text
D = D_{k+1}(Pi_j),
P = P_{k+1}(X).
```

Let `B <= D x P` be the relative Brunnian orbit subgroup generated by
conjugates of the new point-pushing generator under the already-detected old
suffix paired subgroup.  The Brunnian orbit criterion says that the row fails
exactly when

```text
B cap ({1} x P)
```

contains a nonidentity element.

Choose a finite quotient

```text
phi:P -> H
```

of minimal order such that the image `B_H <= D x H` still has nontrivial
vertical kernel

```text
V_H = B_H cap ({1} x H).
```

Assume the unique minimal normal subgroup of `H` is nonabelian:

```text
M = Soc(H) ~= S^r
```

for a nonabelian finite simple group `S`.

## Theorem

In this situation:

1. `V_H=M`;
2. `C_H(M)=1`;
3. `H` embeds faithfully into

   ```text
   Aut(S) wr Omega
   ```

   for a transitive permutation group `Omega <= Sym(r)`;
4. the row modulo `M` succeeds;
5. the whole nonabelian failure is exactly a nontrivial `S`-coordinate value
   of a detector-orbit relation in this wreath embedding.

## Proof

### The Vertical Kernel Is The Monolith

Replacing `H` by the action projection of `B_H`, we may assume `H` is
generated by the transported orbit labels.  Then `V_H` is normal in `H`.

By minimality of `H`, every proper normal quotient of `H` kills the vertical
failure.  Equivalently, the image of `V_H` in every proper quotient `H/N` is
trivial, so

```text
V_H <= N
```

for every nontrivial normal subgroup `N normal H`.  Thus `H` is monolithic and
`1 != V_H <= M`.

Since `V_H` is itself nontrivial and normal in `H`, minimality of `M` gives

```text
V_H=M.
```

### The Quotient Modulo M Succeeds

The image of the vertical kernel in `H/M` is trivial:

```text
V_H M/M = 1.
```

Hence

```text
B_{H/M} cap ({1} x H/M) = 1.
```

So the one-new-strand extension has no Brunnian obstruction after quotienting
by `M`.  The nonabelian failure is exactly a lift obstruction across

```text
1 -> M -> H -> H/M -> 1.
```

### Simple-Power Monolith And Wreath Embedding

A finite nonabelian minimal normal subgroup is a direct product

```text
M ~= S^r
```

of isomorphic nonabelian finite simple groups.  Conjugation by `H` permutes
the `r` simple factors.  This action is transitive: otherwise the product of
the factors in one orbit would be a proper nontrivial normal subgroup of `H`
inside `M`.

The centralizer `C_H(M)` is normal in `H`.  If it were nontrivial, uniqueness
of the minimal normal subgroup would force `M <= C_H(M)`.  But

```text
C_M(M)=Z(M)=1
```

because `S` is nonabelian simple.  Therefore `C_H(M)=1`.

The faithful conjugation action embeds `H` into `Aut(M)`, and

```text
Aut(M) ~= Aut(S) wr Sym(r).
```

Since the induced factor action is transitive, the image lies in

```text
Aut(S) wr Omega
```

for the transitive permutation group `Omega <= Sym(r)` induced by `H`.

## Wreath-Coordinate Relation-Lift Form

Let the detector orbit generators be indexed by a finite set `O`, and let

```text
F_O=<t_o:o in O>.
```

There are maps

```text
mu_D:F_O -> N_D,
mu_H:F_O -> H <= Aut(S) wr Omega.
```

Modulo `M`, the row succeeds.  Therefore every detector relation has lifted
value in the simple-power base:

```text
r in ker(mu_D)  =>  mu_H(r) in M=S^r.
```

Write

```text
mu_H(r)=(s_1(r),...,s_r(r)).
```

The nonabelian-chief failure is exactly the existence of a detector relation
`r in ker(mu_D)` and a coordinate `a` such that

```text
s_a(r) != 1.
```

Because `V_H=M`, the normal closure of these relation values under `H` is all
of `S^r`.  Equivalently, after choosing one coordinate, the normal closure of
the coordinate values under the factor stabilizer generates the simple group
`S`.

Conversely, any such nontrivial coordinate relation gives a nontrivial
vertical element of `B_H`, hence a one-new-strand Brunnian witness by the
orbit criterion.

## Consequence For The Fork

The nonabelian branch is no longer an arbitrary nonabelian quotient problem.
It is exactly:

```text
a detector-orbit relation whose transported lift has a nontrivial S-coordinate
inside a transitive simple-power wreath embedding.
```

Together with the abelian-chief reductions, the final point-pushing fork is:

1. central stem multiplier/coinvariant tails;
2. noncentral irreducible relation-module tails;
3. nonabelian simple-wreath coordinate relation-lift tails.

Rule out all three uniformly for finite YBE point-pushing action images and
outcome A follows from the product-prefix criterion.  Construct one explicit
infinite tail of any one type and outcome B follows after right stabilization.
