# Action-image law obstruction

Date: 2026-05-28

This note records an important constraint on the pure-braid law framework.

## Fixed finite image principle

Suppose a free word `w(x_1,...,x_k)` is embedded into a braid group by choosing
braids `b_1,...,b_k` and setting

```text
beta = w(b_1,...,b_k).
```

For any fixed finite YBE solution `X` and braid index `n`, the images of
`b_1,...,b_k` in `Sym(X^n)` generate a finite group `H`.  If `w` is a law on
`H`, then `rho_{X,n}(beta)=1`.

The code now records this as an exact certificate, not just as prose.  The
helper

```text
law_braid_action_certificate(X, w, arity)
```

embeds `w` through the standard pure braids `A_{r+1,k+1}`, generates the
finite action-image subgroup `H <= Sym(X^{k+1})`, evaluates `w` directly in
`H`, and compares that result with the direct braid action of the embedded
word.  The equality of these two computations is a formal check that the
fixed-image law principle is being applied to the same braid action.

This explains why a counterexample B cannot merely use law words in a fixed
finite set of braid-action generators.  The action image itself is a finite
group and therefore satisfies sufficiently late universal finite-group laws.

## Escape hatch

The B formulation allows `q_j -> infinity`.  Then the finite action groups

```text
H_j <= Sym(X^{q_j})
```

generated by the relevant pure braid images may grow with `j`.  A sequence
`w_j` that is eventually a law for each fixed finite group need not be a law
on the moving groups `H_j`.  This is the only apparent way the pure-braid law
framework could still produce B.

Thus a real counterexample must prove two facts simultaneously:

- the finite-G Artin longitude data is eventually trivial for every fixed
  finite group `G`;
- the same words are not laws on the moving finite action groups
  `rho_{X,q_j}(P_{q_j})`.

## Executable audit

`src/ybe_domination/action_images.py` implements finite permutation images and
subgroup generation.  `tools/run_action_image_law_audit.py` compares direct
braid action against free-word evaluation in the subgroup generated by pure
braid images through `law_braid_action_certificate()`.

The unit tests check both sides of the barrier on the dihedral quandle:

- the exponent word `x^12` is a law on the relevant fixed image subgroup and
  the embedded braid acts trivially;
- the commutator word is not a law on the moving `q=3` image subgroup and the
  embedded braid acts nontrivially.

The generated report is `proofs/action_image_law_audit.json`.
