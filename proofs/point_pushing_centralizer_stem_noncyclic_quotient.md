# Point-Pushing Centralizer-Stem Noncyclic Quotient

Date: 2026-05-30

This note refines
`proofs/point_pushing_centralizer_stem_multiplier_tail.md`.

It does not prove outcome A or B.  It removes cyclic quotient rows from the
centralizer-stem multiplier branch.

## Setup

Use the centralizer-stem notation:

```text
N = <<t>>_H,
M <= Z(N) cap [N,N],
Q = N/M.
```

The extension

```text
1 -> M -> N -> Q -> 1
```

is central and stem.

## Theorem

The quotient `Q=N/M` is not cyclic.

Equivalently, every surviving centralizer-stem multiplier tail must have a
noncyclic finite quotient `N/M`.

## Proof

Suppose `Q` were cyclic.  Choose `x in N` whose image generates `Q`.  Since
`M` is central in `N`, every element of `N` has the form

```text
x^a m
```

with `m in M`.  For two such elements,

```text
(x^a m)(x^b n) = x^{a+b}mn = (x^b n)(x^a m),
```

again because `M<=Z(N)`.  Hence `N` is abelian.

Therefore `[N,N]=1`, contradicting the stem condition

```text
M <= [N,N]
```

with `M` nontrivial.  Thus `Q` cannot be cyclic.  QED.

## Consequence For The Fork

The centralizer-stem multiplier branch is not a disguised cyclic multiplier
tail.  A surviving row must have:

```text
Q=N/M noncyclic,
M a quotient of H_2(Q,Z),
Q ambient-normally generated by the bounded-order image of t.
```

The obstruction must therefore use at least two transported conjugate
directions of the newest point-pushing generator after passing to `N/M`.

The follow-up `proofs/point_pushing_centralizer_stem_transport_split.md`
separates whether the bounded generator image internally normally generates
`N/M`, or whether those extra directions must come from ambient transport
outside the internal normal closure.

## Audit Hook

The helper

```text
point_pushing_centralizer_stem_multiplier_audit(...)
```

now records whether `Q=N/M` is cyclic.  A row is labeled

```text
centralizer_stem_multiplier_tail
```

only when the stem condition holds and the quotient is noncyclic.
