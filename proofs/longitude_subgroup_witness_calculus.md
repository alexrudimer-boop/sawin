# Longitude subgroup witness calculus

Date: 2026-05-29

This note upgrades the homomorphism and product lemmas for longitude-value
subgroups from subgroup-level statements to explicit certificate-level
statements.  It is not the Master Local-Minimal Residual Theorem, but it
removes one more source of hidden finite enumeration from the positive local
detector route.

## Witness Format

For a finite group `G`, braid `beta in B_n`, and recursive Artin longitudes
`L_i(beta)`, a witness for an element of

```text
V_beta(G) =
< phi(L_i(beta)) : phi:F_n -> G, 1<=i<=n >
```

is a finite word whose letters are

```text
(phi, i, epsilon),
epsilon in {+1,-1}.
```

Here `phi` is recorded by the `n`-tuple

```text
(phi(x_1),...,phi(x_n)).
```

The witness evaluates to

```text
phi_1(L_{i_1}(beta))^{epsilon_1}
...
phi_r(L_{i_r}(beta))^{epsilon_r}.
```

Different letters may use different assignments.  This is essential: a word
in the subgroup generated by all longitude values is not required to come
from one common assignment.

## Chart Conjugation

Let `c in G`, and let a witness `W` evaluate to `g in V_beta(G)`.  Conjugate
each assignment in `W` by

```text
phi^c(x_j) = c phi(x_j) c^-1.
```

Then the resulting witness evaluates to

```text
c g c^-1.
```

This is the certificate-level normality of `V_beta(G)`.  It is the mechanism
used in `proofs/chart_transport_collapse.md`: finite chart-conjugate copies of
an elementary generator do not need separate endpoint-longitude proofs once an
orbit representative has a witness.

## Homomorphism Pushforward

Let

```text
f:G -> H
```

be a finite group homomorphism.  If a witness `W` in `G` evaluates to
`g in V_beta(G)`, then applying `f` to every entry in every assignment gives a
witness `f_* W` in `H`, and

```text
eval_H(f_* W) = f(g).
```

This is immediate because every longitude value is a group word in the
assignment entries and `f` preserves multiplication and inversion.  Thus
functoriality of `V_beta(-)` is certificate-level: a proof can push a
displayed endpoint or label witness through a quotient or normalized holonomy
map without enumerating either subgroup.

## Direct Product Assembly

Let

```text
G = G_1 x ... x G_r.
```

Suppose, for each factor, a witness `W_j` evaluates to

```text
g_j in V_beta(G_j).
```

Embed each letter of `W_j` in the product group by using its factor
assignment in coordinate `j` and the all-identity assignments in every other
coordinate.  Concatenating these embedded factor witnesses gives a product
witness `W` in `G`, and

```text
eval_G(W) = (g_1,...,g_r).
```

This is the explicit-word version of

```text
V_beta(G_1 x ... x G_r)
  =
V_beta(G_1) x ... x V_beta(G_r).
```

The empty product gives the empty witness and the identity element `()`.

## Role In The Local Detector Route

The sharp obstruction theorem needs one finite group, not a list of informal
branch groups.  The witness calculus says that if each branch endpoint,
Green kernel-block label, Schutzenberger label, product-label, or unit
holonomy label is displayed by a longitude subgroup witness in a fixed
factor group, then the tuple of all labels is displayed by one witness in the
single fixed product detector group.

Likewise, if a branch proof first constructs a witness in a totalized label
group and then passes to a quotient or normalized holonomy group, the
homomorphism pushforward gives the descended witness explicitly.

This still does not prove that the required local witnesses exist for
arbitrary local-minimal intervals.  The remaining Master Local-Minimal
Residual Theorem must construct such witnesses, or another fixed detector
readout, uniformly in braid index `n`.

## Executable Form

The code exposes:

```text
evaluate_longitude_subgroup_witness(group,n,beta,witness)
conjugate_longitude_subgroup_witness(group,c,witness)
conjugate_longitude_subgroup_witness_audit(group,n,beta,c,witness)
pushforward_longitude_subgroup_witness(f,witness)
pushforward_longitude_subgroup_witness_audit(f,n,beta,witness)
direct_product_longitude_subgroup_witness(groups,n,factor_witnesses)
direct_product_longitude_subgroup_witness_audit(groups,n,beta,factor_witnesses)
```

The conjugation audit checks that the conjugated witness evaluates to
`c g c^-1`.  The pushforward audit checks that the pushed witness evaluates
to the homomorphic image of the source witness value.  The product audit checks
that the assembled product witness evaluates to the tuple of factor witness
values.  Regression tests cover chart conjugation in `S_3`, a quotient
`C_6 -> C_3`, a product `C_2 x C_3`, and the empty product.
