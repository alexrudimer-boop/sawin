# Point-Pushing Internal Stem Abelianization Bound

Date: 2026-05-30

This note refines the internal-generation side of
`proofs/point_pushing_centralizer_stem_transport_split.md`.

It does not prove outcome A or B.  It records the abelianization constraint
forced by a bounded internal normal generator.

## Setup

Use the centralizer-stem quotient notation:

```text
N = <<t>>_H,
M <= Z(N) cap [N,N],
Q = N/M,
q = image(t) in Q.
```

Assume the internal-generation side:

```text
<<q>>_Q = Q.
```

The bounded normal-generator theorem gives

```text
ord(q) | ord(t) | B_X.
```

## Theorem

The abelianization `Q_ab=Q/[Q,Q]` is cyclic and generated by the image of `q`.
Consequently

```text
|Q_ab| | ord(q) | B_X,
```

and

```text
exp(Q_ab) | B_X.
```

## Proof

The image of the normal closure of `q` in the abelianization is the subgroup
generated by the single element `q_ab`, because all conjugates become equal in
an abelian quotient.  Since `q` normally generates `Q`, its image generates
`Q_ab`.

Therefore `Q_ab` is cyclic, generated by `q_ab`.  The order of `q_ab` divides
the order of `q`, which divides `ord(t)`, which divides `B_X`.  For a cyclic
group the order and exponent are the same, so the displayed divisibility
claims follow.  QED.

## Consequence For The Fork

An internal bounded normal-generator centralizer-stem quotient cannot have
unbounded abelianization.  Any infinite internal-generation tail must escape
through the perfect/commutator part of `Q` or through Schur-multiplier growth,
while its abelianization remains cyclic of bounded order.

The transport-orbit branch is not covered by this theorem: there the image of
one bounded generator need not generate `Q_ab`; the ambient transported orbit
can create additional abelian directions.
