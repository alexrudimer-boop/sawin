# Point-Pushing Cyclic Tail Closure

Date: 2026-05-30

This note closes the cyclic p-power branch isolated in
`proofs/point_pushing_cyclic_p_power_tail.md`.

It does not prove outcome A or B.  It removes two of the monolithic tail
types from the negative fork: cyclic prime escape and cyclic p-power depth
escape cannot occur in an infinite point-pushing product-prefix obstruction
for a fixed finite YBE solution.

## Setup

Let `X` be a finite bijective set-theoretic YBE solution.  For arity `k`, let

```text
P_k(X)=<h_1,...,h_k>
```

be the point-pushing action image, where

```text
h_i = rho_{X,k+1}(A_{i,k+1}).
```

Let

```text
B_X = ord(rho_{X,2}(sigma_1^2)).
```

This is a finite integer depending only on `X`.

## Theorem

Every cyclic quotient of every point-pushing action image `P_k(X)` has order
dividing `B_X`.

Consequently, no infinite product-prefix first-failure tail can have cyclic
p-power monolithic quotients.  In particular, both cyclic prime escape and
cyclic p-power depth escape are impossible.

## Proof

The standard pure generator

```text
A_{i,k+1}
```

is braid-conjugate to `sigma_i^2`.  Therefore its image under any braid action
has the same order as the image of `sigma_i^2`.

In the `X`-action on `X^{k+1}`, the braid `sigma_i^2` acts as
`rho_{X,2}(sigma_1^2)` on the two adjacent coordinates and trivially on the
others.  Hence

```text
ord(h_i) = B_X
```

or at least divides `B_X`, for every `i` and every `k`.

Now let

```text
q:P_k(X)->C
```

be a cyclic quotient.  The group `C` is generated by the elements
`q(h_i)`.  Each `q(h_i)` has order dividing `B_X`, so the exponent of the
cyclic group `C` divides `B_X`.  A cyclic group's exponent equals its order.
Therefore

```text
|C| divides B_X.
```

This bound is uniform in `k`.

Now consider a product-prefix monolithic failure with cyclic quotient
`C_{p^e}`.  Monolithic compression gives

```text
p^e > b(j).
```

But the theorem gives

```text
p^e | B_X.
```

Once `b(j) >= B_X`, this is impossible.  Since `b(j)->infinity`, no infinite
tail can remain in the cyclic p-power branch.  QED.

## Consequence For B

The cyclic p-power cases from
`proofs/point_pushing_cyclic_p_power_tail.md` are eliminated from the final
negative fork:

```text
cyclic prime escape       impossible,
cyclic p-power depth      impossible.
```

The remaining monolithic B tail types are now:

1. central stem depth;
2. noncentral module prime escape;
3. noncentral module dimension escape;
4. noncentral bounded-module centralizer-layer escape;
5. nonabelian simple-factor escape;
6. nonabelian multiplicity escape.

Rule out these six uniformly for finite YBE point-pushing action images and
outcome A follows from the product-prefix criterion.  Construct one unbounded
tail of any one type and outcome B follows after right stabilization.

```text

```

the theorem above supplies the all-arity argument by braid conjugacy.
