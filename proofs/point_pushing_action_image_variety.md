# Point-pushing action-image variety reduction

Date: 2026-05-30

This note follows `proofs/sawin_last_strand_law_reduction.md` and
`proofs/fixed_variety_barrier.md`.  It does not prove outcome A or B.  It
rephrases the final point-pushing law test as a moving action-image variety
condition.

## Setup

Let `X` be a finite bijective set-theoretic YBE solution.  For each `k>=1`,
put `n=k+1` and define

```text
P_k(X) =
< rho_{X,n}(A_{1,n}), ..., rho_{X,n}(A_{k,n}) >
<= Sym(X^n).
```

This is the finite point-pushing action image in degree `k+1`.

For a finite group `G`, write `var(G)` for the group variety generated by
`G`, equivalently the class of finite groups satisfying every word law of
`G`.

## Theorem

For a fixed integer `m`, the following are equivalent.

1. Every `S_m`-law point-pushing braid acts trivially on `X`:

   ```text
   for every k>=1 and every w in Law_k(S_m),
   rho_{X,k+1}(iota_{k+1}(w))=1.
   ```

2. Every point-pushing action image lies in the variety generated by `S_m`:

   ```text
   P_k(X) in var(S_m)
   ```

   for every `k>=1`.

Proof.  Let

```text
h_i = rho_{X,k+1}(A_{i,k+1})
```

be the marked generators of `P_k(X)`.

Assume (2).  If `w in Law_k(S_m)`, then `w` is a law on every group in
`var(S_m)`, in particular on `P_k(X)`.  Therefore

```text
w(h_1,...,h_k)=1,
```

which is exactly the action of `iota_{k+1}(w)` on `X^{k+1}`.

Conversely assume (1).  To prove `P_k(X) in var(S_m)`, let

```text
u(y_1,...,y_r)
```

be any law of `S_m`, and choose arbitrary elements

```text
a_1,...,a_r in P_k(X).
```

Since the `h_i` generate `P_k(X)`, choose words

```text
v_1,...,v_r in F_k
```

with

```text
v_l(h_1,...,h_k)=a_l.
```

The substituted word

```text
U(x_1,...,x_k)=u(v_1(x),...,v_r(x))
```

is a `k`-variable law of `S_m`, because every assignment of the `x_i` in
`S_m` makes each `v_l(x)` an element of `S_m`, and `u` is a law on `S_m`.
By (1),

```text
U(h_1,...,h_k)=1.
```

But this is exactly

```text
u(a_1,...,a_r)=1.
```

Thus every law of `S_m` is a law on `P_k(X)`, so
`P_k(X) in var(S_m)`.  QED.

## Consequence For A

By the last-strand law reduction and symmetric detector reduction, Sawin
finite-rack domination for `X` is equivalent to the existence of one `m` such
that

```text
P_k(X) in var(S_m)        for every k>=1.
```

This is a weaker-looking condition than giving explicit recursive-longitude
endpoint witnesses, but for the last-strand law formulation it is exactly
equivalent.

## Consequence For B

A negative proof may be given by constructing, for every `j`, a degree `k_j`
such that

```text
P_{k_j}(X) notin var(S_j),
```

together with an explicit separating law and generator-word substitution.
Concretely, one needs:

1. a word `u_j` which is a law on `S_j`;
2. elements `a_1,...,a_r in P_{k_j}(X)` with

   ```text
   u_j(a_1,...,a_r) != 1;
   ```

3. words `v_l in F_{k_j}` representing the `a_l` in the marked generators
   `rho(A_{i,k_j+1})`;
4. the substituted word

   ```text
   w_j = u_j(v_1,...,v_r),
   ```

   which is a `k_j`-variable law on `S_j` but satisfies

   ```text
   w_j(rho(A_{1,k_j+1}),...,rho(A_{k_j,k_j+1})) != 1.
   ```

Then `iota_{k_j+1}(w_j)` is the required point-pushing mover invisible to
`S_j`.  Symmetric tower monotonicity upgrades this to eventual invisibility
to every fixed finite group.

## Existing Diagnostics

The bounded helper

```text
short_law_escaping_variety(S_m, P_k(X), arity, max_length)
```

can find finite evidence that a computed action image escapes `var(S_m)`.
Such evidence is not yet a B proof: the final theorem still needs a symbolic
all-`j` family and the representing substitutions in the marked
point-pushing generators.

Similarly, `law_braid_action_certificate(...)` verifies, for one supplied
word, that evaluation on the pure-generator action image matches the direct
braid action on `X^{k+1}`.

This note therefore converts the final problem into a clean moving-variety
question:

```text
either all point-pushing action images P_k(X) lie in one fixed finite variety,
or they escape every fixed symmetric variety in a way that yields explicit
S_j-law point-pushing movers.
```
