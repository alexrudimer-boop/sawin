# Green Detector Group Candidate

Date: 2026-05-28

This note makes the current Green/corridor detector candidate explicit as
ordinary finite groups.  It does not prove the Green residual detection
lemma; it supplies the concrete finite group factors that such a proof would
use.

## Finite Factors

For a finite bijective YBE solution `X`, let `tau_x(y)=pr_1 R(x,y)` be the
left coordinate-action maps.  The Green audit forms regular Green
`R`-classes in the finite transformation monoid generated by the `tau_x`.

There are two finite group layers.

First, if `C` is a regular Green `R`-class, then every transformation in `C`
has the same kernel partition `K_C`.  A retained label `x` with
`s tau_x in C` preserves this kernel and induces a permutation on the finite
block set.  The detector uses the full symmetric group on this block set,
not merely the actual induced image group, because bounded audits show the
actual image group can be too small while the full symmetric block group
detects the relevant commutator movers.

Second, the right stabilizer of `C` in the finite coordinate-action monoid
acts on `C` by permutations.  This is the Schutzenberger action.  The code now
exposes these actions as actual finite groups:

```text
schutzenberger_action_groups(X)
schutzenberger_groups(X)
```

The side-opposite solution `X^op=P R P` gives the right-coordinate analogue.
Thus the current two-sided factor list is

```text
two_sided_green_detector_groups(X)
```

which consists of:

- full symmetric groups on left and right Green kernel-block quotients;
- left and right Schutzenberger action groups;
- duplicates only when the concrete finite group table is literally the same.

The single product group is

```text
two_sided_green_detector_product(X).
```

By the direct-product detector lemma, this is one finite group suitable for
the sharp obstruction theorem's notation.  It depends only on the finite
interval data and is independent of braid degree `n`.

## Why Concrete Degree Matters

The code deliberately does not deduplicate merely isomorphic factors.  A left
Schutzenberger action and a right Schutzenberger action may both be cyclic in
abstract group theory but act on different finite Green classes and have
different concrete permutation tables.  Keeping both factors is harmless:
their direct product is still finite and independent of `n`, and identity
longitude data in the product is equivalent to identity longitude data in
each factor.

## Current Fixture

For the size-three affine commutator candidate, the explicit two-sided Green
detector factors have orders

```text
2, 3, 6.
```

Here the order-`6` factor is the full symmetric kernel-block group, while the
order-`3` and order-`2` factors are Schutzenberger actions from the two
sides.  Their direct product has order `36`.

This is a construction and convention check, not a proof that these factors
detect all Green/corridor residual holonomy.  The remaining theorem-level
task is:

> Prove that residual Green/corridor branch choices factor through recursive
> Artin longitude evaluations in the finite group
> `two_sided_green_detector_product(X)` multiplied by the already-known
> branch factors, or construct a normalized-law sequence escaping every such
> finite group.
