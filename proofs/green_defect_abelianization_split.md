# Green defect abelianization split

Date: 2026-05-29

This note follows `proofs/artin_defect_abelianization_barrier.md`.  It does
not prove the Master Local-Minimal Residual Theorem.  It separates the
remaining Green/Schutzenberger defect-kernel endpoint into an abelian quotient
target and a commutator-subgroup target.

The point is to keep the A-route honest.  Artin permutation defects cannot see
abelian defect-kernel data, but ordinary recursive longitudes can.  Therefore
the abelian part must be treated as a finite abelian longitude problem, not as
an Artin-defect problem.

## Setup

Let `Def_C` be the finite normal first-output defect kernel inside a
Green/Schutzenberger observer group `U_C`.  The previous reductions prove:

```text
U_C/Def_C
```

is rack-detected, and every elementary defect is a potential coboundary

```text
d(a,q)=eta(q^a) eta(q)^-1
```

inside `Def_C`.

Let

```text
Com_C = [Def_C,Def_C]
```

be the commutator subgroup of the defect kernel.  Since `Def_C` is normal in
`U_C`, `Com_C` is characteristic in `Def_C` and hence normal in `U_C`.
Therefore the finite quotient

```text
U_C / Com_C
```

is defined, and the image

```text
AbDef_C = Def_C / Com_C
```

is a finite abelian subgroup of that quotient.

## Split criterion

For a residual braid `beta`, let

```text
D_C(beta) in Def_C
```

be the transported defect-kernel endpoint.  Then:

```text
D_C(beta) in V_beta(Def_C)
```

can be attacked by first projecting to the abelianized defect target
`AbDef_C`.

The necessary abelian target is:

```text
bar D_C(beta) in V_beta(AbDef_C),
```

where `bar D_C(beta)` is the image of the endpoint in `Def_C/Com_C`.

If this abelian target holds and a lift of the endpoint differs from a
displayed abelian longitude value by an element of `Com_C`, then the remaining
problem is a commutator-subgroup endpoint in `Com_C`.

This is only a reduction.  It does not claim that the abelian target
automatically holds.  It identifies the finite abelian group in which the
ordinary longitude equations must be proved.

## Row-level form

Project the finite potential to `U_C/Com_C`.  For every row:

```text
bar d(a,q)=bar eta(q^a) bar eta(q)^-1.
```

The image of `Def_C` in the quotient is abelian, so any transported endpoint
product has an abelianized potential accounting: the endpoint depends only on
the signed total contribution of the projected potential labels.  A proof of
the abelian part must identify that signed contribution as an ordinary
recursive-longitude value, or prove that it cancels for every residual braid
under consideration.

This is exactly where the Artin-defect-only route failed: Artin permutation
defect values project trivially to `AbDef_C`.

The follow-up note `proofs/abelian_longitude_image_criterion.md` makes this
abelian target exact.  For a finite abelian group `A`, if `M_beta=(m_ij)` is
the abelianized recursive-longitude exponent matrix, then

```text
V_beta(A)=< a^{m_ij} : a in A, i,j positions >.
```

Thus the projected defect endpoint in `AbDef_C` must be displayed in this
matrix-generated subgroup, or shown to cancel under the residual hypotheses.

## Consequence for the A-route

The remaining Green route now has two explicit layers.

1. Abelian layer.  Prove the projected endpoint lies in
   `V_beta(AbDef_C)`, equivalently in the finite subgroup generated by
   `a^{m_ij}` for the entries of the abelianized recursive-longitude matrix.
   This may use pairwise-linking style arguments, endpoint cancellation, or
   detector-lift labels.  It cannot use Artin permutation defects alone.
2. Commutator layer.  After the abelian layer is killed, the residual endpoint
   lies in `Com_C`.  There the Artin-defect route may again become relevant,
   since Artin permutation defects naturally land in commutator subgroups.

Both groups are finite and depend only on the local interval.  Therefore this
split preserves the sharp obstruction requirement that the final detector
group be independent of braid index.

## Counterexample consequence

A B-route must now defeat the abelian layer or the commutator layer uniformly.
A single nontrivial abelian row defect does not suffice: it may still be an
ordinary recursive-longitude value.  To become outcome B, any failure must be
upgraded to a normalized-law sequence invisible to every finite group while
moving a residual tuple.

The code records this split through:

```text
green_defect_abelianization_split_audit(...)
schutzenberger_defect_abelianization_split_audits(...)
kernel_block_defect_abelianization_split_audits(...)
```

The audit computes `Com_C=[Def_C,Def_C]`, constructs the quotient
`U_C/Com_C`, records the finite abelian image of `Def_C`, and verifies the
projected potential coboundary identity row by row.  On the affine stress row,
the abelianized defect image has order `3`, and `18` completed rows have
nontrivial abelianized defect.
